About me

I'm a Computer Science student at Tanta University, Faculty of Science, with over a year of hands-on experience building Flutter apps for Android and iOS.

I like structuring projects properly: MVVM, separated data and UI layers, and state management that matches the size of the app, whether that's Provider, Cubit, or BLoC. I've also connected apps to Firebase backends and public REST APIs.

I care about the details users actually feel: loading states, dark mode, and screens that behave on every device size.
